# Experiment design proposal: SDL 4 — Polymers

## Objective

Autonomous polymer discovery and formulation: optimizing monomer choice, composition, molecular weight, and processing to hit target mechanical, thermal, and transport properties. Campaigns span continuous composition and process variables with discrete monomer/architecture choices, and rely on property prediction plus automated synthesis and characterization (GPC, DSC, rheology). Measurements are moderately to highly expensive and the structure-property map is noisy, favoring sample-efficient multi-objective optimization with physically informed surrogates.

## Risks & lessons from source domains

**Risks (negative results):**
- Traditional design methods are restricted to a narrow design space, which the data-driven loop is meant to widen. [src:acgold_69fd03b3ab15]
- Comprehensive open datasets remain scarce despite decades of research, which is itself the bottleneck. [src:acgold_45436743f63f]
- There is no consensus universal representation for macromolecules; the right descriptor is sensitive to the property being predicted. [src:acgold_5e6d32360cf7]
- Coverage is bounded by the chosen library; properties outside the enumerated chemical space are not represented. [src:acgold_11f7152e6b04]

**What worked (positive lessons):**
- Pairing experimental automation with ML lets you accelerate design even when starting from only a small subset of experiments. [src:acgold_69fd03b3ab15]
- Complex multi-component formulations make design intricate, so constraining the space up front is necessary. [src:acgold_69fd03b3ab15]
- A shared, literature-curated open dataset is the prerequisite that unblocks ML-accelerated formulation design in a field that lacked one. [src:acgold_45436743f63f]
- Designing and optimizing these formulations is an iterative trial-and-error process that data-driven models aim to shortcut. [src:acgold_45436743f63f]
- Coupling first-principles simulation with Bayesian optimization makes compositional search over a huge space tractable. [src:acgold_65692bf39579]
- An oversampling variant of Bayesian learning helps when the informative region is sparse or imbalanced. [src:acgold_65692bf39579]
- Without mechanistic insight into the system, blind compositional modification is unfeasible, so interpretability is part of the method, not an add-on. [src:acgold_65692bf39579]
- Casting the search as evolutionary Monte Carlo lets you tune exploration vs exploitation with a single temperature knob while keeping GA-style diversity. [src:acgold_1e9ddd3dc480]
- Encoding secondary requirements as hard lower-bound constraints keeps optimized candidates physically admissible. [src:acgold_1e9ddd3dc480]
- The approach inherits the vastness of the combinatorial space, so results depend heavily on the seed library and mutation operators chosen. [src:acgold_1e9ddd3dc480]
- Making feature-engineering knowledge accessible is itself a lever for accelerating discovery. [src:acgold_5e6d32360cf7]
- Representation choice should be treated as a tunable design decision, not a fixed preprocessing step. [src:acgold_5e6d32360cf7]
- A tiered set of theory levels lets you trade compute cost against accuracy and produce calibrated reference labels. [src:acgold_11f7152e6b04]
- Recording CPU time and storage per calculation makes the cost of high-fidelity labels explicit for downstream budgeting. [src:acgold_11f7152e6b04]
