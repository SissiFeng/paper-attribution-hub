### Problem

### Hypothesis
- Automated mechanical polishing using a robotic arm can improve the surface quality of corroded electrodes and achieve results similar to manual polishing.

### Evidence
- The integrals of the CV plots for different polishing patterns were plotted against polishing time, showing that all patterns performed similarly within the error range, and the performance of robotic polishing was similar to manual polishing with no statistical differences after polishing for 60 seconds.

### Conclusion
- The automated electrode polishing system successfully improves the surface quality of corroded electrodes, and the effect of the polishing pattern was found to be insignificant, enabling fully automated electrochemistry experiments.
