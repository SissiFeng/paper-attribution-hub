## Source Summary
A 3D deep learning framework rapidly segments catalyst and gas diffusion layers, achieving porosity accuracy within 2%, distinguishes microporous layers, automates extraction of key metrics, and provides interactive 3D visualization for on-site analysis.

## Abstract
Highlights 3D deep learning framework segments catalyst and gas diffusion layers in seconds. Porosity-calibrated training achieves segmentation porosity accuracy within 2%. Multiclass model distinguishes microporous layer from gas diffusion substrate. Automated pipeline extracts porosity, pore size, permeability, and tortuosity metrics. Interactive 3D visualization enables on-site exploration of segmented volumes.
