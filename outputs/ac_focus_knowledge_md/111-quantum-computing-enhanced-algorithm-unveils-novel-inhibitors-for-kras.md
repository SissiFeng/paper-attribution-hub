### Method
- A hybrid quantum-classical algorithm integrating a Quantum Circuit Born Machine (QCBM) with a classical Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) network, enhanced with a reward function to generate drug-like small molecules.

### Problem
- Discovery of small molecules with therapeutic potential, specifically for KRAS protein which is a notorious target in drug discovery.

### Hypothesis
- A quantum-classical generative model can be used to design novel KRAS inhibitors with improved efficacy.

### Evidence
- Two molecules, ISM061-018-2 and ISM061-22, demonstrated effective engagement with KRAS, with ISM061-018-2 exhibiting a binding affinity to KRAS-G12D at 1.4µM.

### Conclusion
- The hybrid quantum-classical algorithm demonstrated the ability to yield experimentally confirmed biological hits, showcasing the practical potential of quantum-assisted drug discovery.
