## Abstract
The critical micelle concentration (CMC) is a fundamental property of surfactants, essential for designing effective formulations across various industries. Traditional methods for measuring CMC, such as pyrene fluorescence assays, are sensitive and accurate but labor-intensive and prone to human and equipment variability. Here, we present a fully automated workflow for CMC determination using a robotic liquid-handling system combined with fluorescence plate reading. The workflow automates all steps from sample preparation to fluorescence measurement and data analysis, allowing for high-throughput, reproducible measurements with minimal human intervention. We validated the system using eight commonly used surfactants, achieving strong agreement with literature values and demonstrating robustness across time, measurement replicates, and independent assays. The workflow was further extended to binary surfactant mixtures using a two-round screening strategy to ensure reliable and precise determination of unknown CMCs through a broad concentration range. In total, mixed CMCs of 95 unique surfactant mixtures were measured, uncovering clear synergistic effects and deviations from classical empirical models such as the Clint equation. This open-source, scalable workflow enables rapid and reliable CMC determination, providing a valuable foundation for formulation development and data-driven surfactant research. © 2025 Elsevier B.V.
