## Abstract
Chemistry experiments often demand significant time and effort, particularly for repetitive tasks. We present ORGANA, an assistive robotic system that automates a variety of lab procedures using AI-driven decision-making and perception tools. ORGANA interacts with chemists via natural language to clarify goals, handle disambiguation, and provide updates about experiments. Additionally, by automating complex tasks and enabling parallel execution, ORGANA reduces physical demand and frees up significant time for scientists, paving the way for more efficient and adaptable lab automation.
