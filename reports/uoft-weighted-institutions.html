How to read the roles.

Last author is inferred from the final position in the ordered author list. Corresponding author comes from explicit corresponding-author metadata and can overlap with first, middle, or last position. Role slices preserve the original paper credit rather than renormalizing it. “Fractional affiliation” divides an author's credit across eligible affiliations; “full affiliation” assigns that credit to every eligible affiliation.
